This observation tower is located at Padang Merdeka or Merdeka Square in the centre of Kota Bharu town, along the riverbank. Visitors can make their ascent to the top of the tower. From this vantage point, visitors can enjoy a 360-degree bird's eye view of Kota Bharu.

## MUSEUM OF ROYAL TRADITIONS AND CUSTOMS (ISTANA JAHAR)

This museum was built in 1887 by Sultan Muhamad, with intricate woodcarvings and elaborate roofs that showcase excellent Kelantanese craftsmanship. The palace has been converted into a museum to share Kelantanese heritage and culture with the world. Here, visitors can learn all about royal births, weddings and other ceremonial occasions.

## ROYAL MUSEUM (ISTANA BATU)

The Royal Museum was built in 1989 under the reign of Sultan Ismail |. Formerly the palace of the crown prince, it was donated to the state to be used as a museum. Today, the richly furnished rooms are a lovely repository of Kelantan royal family regalia and the museum disseminates information on the state's royal family, their personal collections and palace decorations.

## KAMPUNG KRAFTANGAN (HANDICRAFT VILLAGE AND CRAFT

Located in the heart of Kota Bharu's cultural zone, the Handicraft Village and Craft Museum provide visitors with the opportunity to see firsthand how the state's famous handicrafts are made. Demonstrations of traditional embroidery, songket weaving, batik printing, silverwork and woodcarving are all carried out by skilled artisans. The Craft Museum houses many fine examples of Kelantanese craftsmanship.

## STATE MUSEUM

The State Museum sits in the building which originally housed the Land Office under the British rule. Converted into a museum today, it houses special exhibits such as traditional arts, crafts and musical instruments. There are even artefacts of ceramics from early trading with China.

## WAKAF CHE YEH NIGHT MARKET

This is the largest market in the state, about 4km from Kota Bharu, and quite close to the Thai border. Functioning as a wet and wholesale market during the day, it comes alive at night, with an assortment of local products ranging from fabrics and clothes to food and local fruits. Whether just wandering around and taking in the local colour or haggling with local traders, a visit here is as entertaining as it is eye-opening.

Situated in the heart of Southeast Asia, Malaysia is a vibrant destination with a diversity of cultures and traditions. This tropical paradise boasts pristine jungles and verdant national parks that are teeming with numerous species of birds and wildlife.

Malaysia's sun-drenched islands and beaches are home to a stunning range of marine life. Its natural wealth is complemented by magnificent modern landmarks and heritage buildings. ## Kelantan

Kelantanese culture is wonderfully unique, from its delicious cuisine to the distinctive Kelantanese Malay dialect spoken throughout the state. It is also home to some of the most ancient archaeological discoveries in Malaysia, including several prehistoric aboriginal settlements. Acknowledged as the Cradle of Malay Culture, Kelantan is a veritable showcase of traditional Malay customs and art forms, which includes wau (giant kite-flying) and wayang kulit (shadow puppetry). Kelantanese handicrafts such as batik, kain songket (woven brocade) and silverware are especially fine and sought after.

## GETTING THERE

Malaysia Airlines, AirAsia, Firefly and Batik Air operate regular flights to Kota Bharu. For those who would like to try out a train journey, Keretapi Tanah Melayu (KTMB) offers a somewhat adventurous journey to several towns such as Gua Musang, Kuala Krai, Tanah Merah, Pasir Mas, Wakaf Bharu and Tumpat. However, passengers must take note that there are no direct train services to Kelantan from Kuala Lumpur. Passengers from Kuala Lumpur will disembark at the Gemas station (in Negeri Sembilan) before boarding the eastbound train to Kelantan. Tickets for both trains (KL - Gemas and Gemas - Kelantan) can be purchased in advance. Check the KTMB website at https://www.ktmb.com.my for details. Travelling by road is an easy option with the availability of frequent and direct bus services to many towns around

Kelantan. Passengers from Kuala Lumpur can board their buses from Terminal Bersepadu Selatan (TBS in short) located at Bandar Tasik Selatan.

In Kota Bharu itself, trishaws are an interesting way to explore the city. Kota Bharu Bus Station, situated at the town centre near Pasar Siti Khadijah, provides connectivity to various places within Kota Bharu. To venture outside of Kota Bharu, travellers can take buses from the Lembah Sireh Bus Terminal. The local train is another interesting way to get around in Kelantan. The train operates from Tumpat in the north to Gua Musang in the south.

## SHOPPING

A bewildering range of handicrafts in various colours, shapes and sizes fill the shelves at Kelantan's souvenir shops. Take your pick from the intricate handicrafts, silverware, fashionable batik sarongs, pareos and trendy bags to the richly-embroidered songket fabric and many more. In many places, visitors can also watch how the crafts are made. Some items such as wau (giant kites) can be custom-made to suit your liking. Duty-free shopping zones are available at Rantau Panjang, Pengkalan Kubor and Bukit Bunga. There are modern shopping centres such as KB Mall, Mydin Tunjong Hypermarket, Tesco and AEON Mall.

## DINING

There is a delightful assortment of local fare and most eateries are open until late at night to cater to the adventurous foodies. Rice, the staple food, is creatively cooked in many ways with the use of different herbs and spices. Local dishes are spicy and pungent and some dishes have Thai influences. The state is also known for its variety of sumptuous desserts. Alternatively, visitors can opt for Western food served at major restaurants and hotels. Fast food outlets are available in major towns.

SITTING BUDDHA TEMPLE (WAT

This is a Thai Buddhist temple in the town of Tumpat, approximately 15km from Kota Bharu, that houses the largest reclining statue of Buddha (or Sleeping Buddha) in Malaysia. This 40m statue was constructed in 1973 with donations from various Buddhist associations in Malaysia and Thailand.

Tumpat is the site of most of the state's Buddhist temples. Tne Wat Machimmaram houses a large Seated Buddha (or Sitting Buddha) that is 30m high and 18m wide, reputedly the largest in Southeast Asia. It is a popular pilgrimage destination for Buddhist devotees, especially during festive and auspicious days.

## SHUI YUE GONG TEMPLE

This Chinese temple in Pulai contains a sacred painting of the Goddess of Mercy or Kuan Yin, estimated to be 500 to 700 years old. Every year, a nine-day festival is held in honour of her birthday, with devotees coming from all around Asia. It is said that the painting was brought from China by one of the early Chinese immigrants.

## MUZIUM WAU KELANTAN (KELANTAN KITE MUSEUM)

The wau or traditional kite museum at Pantai Sri Tujuh is a complete showcase of kite culture, providing information on the history and development of these giant kites. The museum has displays of the various types of kites, and also teaches visitors about the age- old traditions in Kelantan and other states in Malaysia, as well as traditional kites from around the world.

## PANTAI CAHAYA BULAN

Just 10km from Kota Bharu, Pantai Cahaya Bulan is a particularly beautiful stretch of white sandy beach that is popular for family outings and picnics. It also has a variety of water sports including parasailing and canoeing, as well as horse riding. Food stalls and restaurants are dotted along the beach, offering fresh barbecued seafood.

## KUALA kOH NATIONAL PARK

Taman Negara is Malaysia's premier and largest national park that is spread over the three states of Pahang, Kelantan and Terengganu. Sprawling over 1,013 sq km, the National Park in Kuala Koh makes up 24% of the total park area. Numerous trails and treks are a great way to get to know the surroundings. Other activities include tubing, rafting, birdwatching and more.

Located along Jalan Hospital, the Kelantan State Art Gallery showcases some of the finest works of art from local artists in a variety of mediums. Regular exhibitions are held from time to time. The art gallery was officially launched on 9 December 2013.

## GUNUNG STONG STATE PARK

The state park features several prominent mountain peaks and one of the highest waterfalls in Malaysia, the seven-tiered Jelawang Waterfall. It is a significant conservation area flourishing with exotic flora and fauna as well as breathtaking 225-millionyear-old limestone caves. Gunung Ayam, the highest peak at 1,504m, is an ideal spot to catch the beautiful sunrise or sunset.

+609 744 3949 (Yayasan Kraftangan Kelantan)

## KAMPUNG LAUT HERITAGE AREA, TUMPAT

Built in the 18th century, Kampung Laut Mosque is hailed as one of the oldest mosques in Southeast Asia. It stands as a landmark for its historical value and unique archaeological features, particularly its wooden structure that was built without the use of nails.In the past, the mosque was prone to floods due to its location by the riverbank. It survived two major floods, the first in 1926 and another in 1966.To preserve and safeguard the structure, the mosque was relocated to Nilam Puri which lies about 20km away. Now, the mosque has been restored and relocated to its original location in Tumpat. History buffs can view original artefacts such as 'beduk' (drum) and 'mimbar' (pulpit).
