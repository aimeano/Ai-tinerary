## MALAYSIA

Immerse yourself in the vibrant heart of Southeast Asia with a visit to Malaysia. Experience the beauty of pristine jungles, verdant national parks, and sun-drenched islands teeming with marine life. Explore modern landmarks and heritage buildings, while indulging in the rich blend of various cultures and traditions. Whether you're a history-lover, nature enthusiast, sun-seeker, or avid shopper, Malaysia has something for everyone. Embark on a journey to discover the country's many alluring attractions!

## Kuala Lumpur

Kuala Lumpur or KL is Malaysia's capital city. It is one of the most vibrant and fast-growing cities in Asia, with exciting places to see along with world-famous landmarks, prestigious hotels and state-of-the-art facilities. This city has something for everyone, the business traveller, families, honeymooners and kids. Visit the glittering Petronas Twin Towers, the world's tallest twin structures and enjoy a leisurely stroll around Dataran Merdeka which gives a glimpse into KL's past. Spend some time at the parks, museums and art galleries or enjoy the adrenaline-rush at the theme parks. KL's status as the fourth among the top ten shopping cities in the world makes shopping a must on your itinerary. Enjoy KL, and leave with memories of a great holiday in Malaysia.

## GETTING THERE

KL is easily accessible by air, sea and land. The main airport in the country is the Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) Terminal 1 which is about one hour's drive from the city. It is complemented by KLIA Terminal 2, the world's largest purpose-built terminal dedicated to low cost carriers. The national carrier Malaysia Airlines is complemented by its subsidiary Firefly and the budget airline AirAsia.

Visitors arriving by train from Ipoh and Butterworth can take the high-speed ETS train service provided by Keretapi Tanah Melayu or KTM. From the airport, visitors can take the KLIA Express and KLIA Transit fast trains for a hassle-free journey to the city. Buses, taxis and car rental services are also available.

## GETTING AROUND

Getting around the city is convenient with the network of trains and buses. KL Sentral Station is the city's modern transportation hub. The services here include KLIA Ekspres/Transit, MRT, LRT, KL Monorail and KTM Komuter trains. In addition, visitors can also get around via the KL Hop On Hop Off Bus Tours.

## SHOPPING

Kuala Lumpur is a paradise for shoppers with something to cater to every taste and budget. The huge shopping malls are stocked with international and designer brands while the specialty outlets offer novelty items and irresistible souvenirs. There are numerous other chic malls around the city such as Mid Valley Megamall and The Gardens, Sunway Putra Mall, Nu Sentral, Berjaya Times Square and MyTown Shopping Centre. For those who want a different shopping experience, there are places like Pasar Seni, Jalan Tunku Abdul Rahman and Petaling Street that offers a mind-boggling range of items.

## DINING

Kuala Lumpur is hailed as the region's food capital for its delectable variety of food and availability of 24-hour restaurants. Be it for enjoying a lavish meal at exclusive restaurants or chilling out at the alfresco cafes, KL offers unbeatable dining experiences. To savour the local delights, visit Kampung Bharu (Malay food), Jalan Alor (Chinese food) and Little India @ Brickfields (Indian food).

## PETRONAS TWIN TOWERS

Standing at a height of 451.9 metres, this gleaming structure of steel and glass is one of the world's tallest buildings. It is the centre piece of the Kuala Lumpur City Centre or KLCC, a prime attraction consisting of the Suria KLCC Shopping Mall, luxurious hotels, a landscaped park and a convention centre. Enjoy the exhilaration of standing at the Skybridge on the 41st Floor and Viewing Deck on the 86th Floor. Admission is on a first-come-firstserved basis.

www.petronastwintowers.com.my

## AQUARIA KLCC Kuala Lumpur Convention Centre

Covering over 60,000 sq ft, Aquaria KLCC is a state of-theart oceanarium. It showcases 5,000 aquatic and land-bound creatures from Malaysia and around the world. Designed by experts, the world-class facility has a 90 metre transparent underwater tunnel for visitors to get a good look at Sand Tiger Sharks, Giant Stingrays and Marine Turtles. Want something more exciting? Watch piranhas during their feeding times, or be a daredevil and join the Cage Rage and Dive with Sharks programmes for a thrilling experience!

www.aquariaklcc.com.my

## KUALA LUMPUR CRAFT COMPLEX

Styled after a Malay village, this complex houses a Craft Boutique, Craft Shop, Artist's Colony and an Amphitheatre. It is a one-stop centre for authentic Malaysian craft products ranging from souvenirs, interior decorations, costume jewellery, textiles, garden and landscape decorations and many more. Art workshops and demonstrations are held for visitors.

kraftangan.gov.my

## MALAYSIA TOURISM CENTRE (MATiC)

Set in a colonial building, MATIC is a one-stop centre for tourists. Enjoy a host of facilities such as a 24-hour tourist police counter, Internet access, tour agencies, currency exchange, hotel reservations as well as souvenir outlets and a chocolate boutique. Constructed in 1935, MATIC was originally a family residence of a wealthy tin miner and rubber estate tycoon. The building has been the venue of several important occasions including the first sitting of Malaysia's parliament.

www.matic.gov.my

## PINTASAN SALOMA (SALOMA BRIDGE)

The Saloma Bridge is a 69-meter pedestrian bridge that connects the KLCC area to Kampung Baru. It only takes 15 minutes to walk across. The bridge is named after Saloma, a singer, actress, and fashion icon known for her performances. The design of the bridge is inspired by the Sirih Junjung, a bouquet used in Malay weddings. The bridge is decorated with LED lights that change every few seconds. Visitors can access the bridge from the Kampung Baru LRT station or from the Public Bank Tower in KLCC.

## KL TOWER

Poised atop Bukit Nanas, KL Tower is a major landmark in the city. Standing at 421m, this telecommunications tower is the seventh tallest in the world and the tallest in Southeast Asia. Highlights include the 276-metre Observation Deck, Sky Box situated 300 m above ground or dining at the ARAS restaurant. The tower is situated in the KL Forest Eco Park.

www.kltower.com.my

## KL FOREST ECO PARK

Formerly known as Bukit Nanas Forest Reserve, this is one of the oldest forest reserves in Malaysia, gazetted in 1906. It is situated at the foothills of KL Tower. This permanent forest reserve beckons with a variety of flora and fauna, a 200 m canopy walk, nature trails and a herb garden. KL Forest Eco Park is open from 8am to 5pm daily. There are two entrances to the park; the KL Tower entrance and the KL Forest Eco Park Head Office

entrance located at Jalan Raja Chulan.

www.forestry.gov.my

## THE EXCHANGE 106 @ TRX

The Exchange 106, formerly known as the TRX Signature Tower, is a 445.5-meter-tall skyscraper in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. It is the second-tallest building in Malaysia and the third-tallest building in Southeast Asia. It is also the second largest skyscraper in Malaysia by floor area with 453,885 m².

## SULTAN ABDUL SAMAD BUILDING

Sultan Abdul Samad Building one of the city's most enduring attractions. Built in 1897, the building is said to have been the finest in the Malay states. It features an imposing porch, graceful horseshoe shaped arches, shiny copper domes and a 41.2 metre clock tower. The Ground Floor of this building houses a Tourist Information Centre.

## DATARAN MERDEKA (MERDEKA SQUARE) Jalan Raja

Dataran Merdeka is one of the most important heritage sites in the city. The Union Jack was lowered here to mark the country's independence from colonial rule. Hence the name Dataran Merdeka, which means 'Independence Square'.

## MASJID JAMEK SULTAN ABDUL SAMAD

## Off Jalan Tun Perak

Built in 1909, Masjid Jamek Sultan Abdul Samad is the city's oldest surviving mosque. It features design elements that are inspired by the Mogul architecture. Cupolas and minarets top the brick walls and arched colonnades, while a 70 feet dome surmounts the prayer hall. This mosque occupies a historic location at the confluence of the Klang and Gombak rivers, which is the birthplace of Kuala Lumpur.

## KWAI CHAI HONG STREET ARTS / MURAL)

Check out Kwai Chai Hong in Kuala Lumpur's Chinatown to experience a slice of history through realistic street art.
