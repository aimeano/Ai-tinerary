## WOODBALL

Jalan Lok Cha Woodball is a popular sport in Perlis. Every year, woodball competitors and enthusiasts from around the world gather here for national and regional-level championships. This unusual sport is a mixture of miniature golf and croquet. It is played with wooden balls, a mallet and a gate. Woodball courses are situated in Kangar and Arau.

+6013 432 9734 (Perlis Woodball Association)

## HARUMANIS MANGO PLANTATION

Perlis is famed for its own variety of mango, known as Harumanis. Literally meaning fragrant (harum) and sweet (manis), this delicious fruit can be savoured by itself or eaten with glutinous or sticky rice. Visitors to the 1,000 acre plantation in Sungai Batu Pahat can learn all about the Harumanis mango. This seasonal fruit can be found between the months of April to June.

+604 938 4466 pertanian.perlis.gov.my

## SNAKE & REPTILE FARM

This farm was established by the Institute of Medical Research to aid in the research and production of antivenom. It showcases more than 200 snakes from 34 species found in Malaysia as well as other parts of the world. Among them are the python and the feared king cobra. The farm also has reptiles such as crocodiles and iguanas. Visitors can have their picture taken with the snakes under the supervision of the handlers.

+604 976 8511

Putra Golf Club is one of the leading golf courses in the country. The scenic course features water hazards, bunkering and occasional ravines to provide ample challenges to golfers. This picturesque course is covered in cowgrass fairways and is set against the backdrop of jungle-clad hills.

## Malaysia

Situated in the heart of Southeast Asia, Malaysia is a vibrant destination with a diversity of cultures and traditions. This tropical paradise boasts pristine jungles and verdant national parks that are teeming with numerous species of birds and wildlife. Malaysia's sun-drenched islands and beaches are home to a stunning range of marine life. Its natural wealth is complemented by magnificent modern landmarks and heritage buildings. There is something to charm every visitor, be it a history-lover, natureenthusiast, sun-seeker or an avid shopper. Explore the length and breadth of the country to discover the many attractions of Malaysia!

## Perlis

Known as the Northern Gem, Perlis is the northern gateway to Malaysia. Despite its small size, Perlis has an abundance of attractions that make it a great destination, be it for adventure-lovers, shopping enthusiasts or holidaymakers in search of a relaxing vacation. Perlis is flanked by Kedah in the south and Thailand (Malaysia's northern neighbor) in the north. The state is characterised by sprawling paddy plains and picturesque views of the countryside, interspersed with rustic villages, limestone outcrops and serene lakes. Its capital city, Kangar, is a great destination with delightful orchards and verdant recreational parks.

## GETTING THERE GT

Perlis is the country's northern gateway. The state shares a border with Thailand, Malaysia's northern neighbour. There is no airport in Perlis, but the North-South Expressway provides easy access from other parts of the country. Visitors can also arrive in Perlis from Thailand. Both the Malaysian and Thai rail companies serve two stations in Perlis; Arau and Padang Besar. For visitors arriving by sea, Kuala Perlis is the main jumping-off point. The ferry services here connect Perlis to the island of Langkawi in Kedah. From places like Kuala Lumpur or Penang, you can hop on the ETS train, enjoying a smooth ride as you make your way towards the serene landscapes of Perlis.

## GETTING AROUND GA

The small size of the state makes getting around in Perlis fairly easy and convenient. The Kangar Bus Station in Jalan Bukit Lagi offers regular shuttle services to towns like Padang Besar, Arau, Kuala Perlis, Pauh, Jitra and Kaki Bukit. Taxis are another convenient way to get around the state.

## SHOPPING S

Perlis is a paradise for bargain-hunters and shopaholics with its duty-free complexes and night markets. Popular

shopping spots include Padang Besar Arcade and Padang Waremart. Here, visitors can find an endless array of things from toys, cookware to clothing. Load your shopping carts with everything from souvenirs to household items!

## DINING D

Visitors should not miss out on the local favourites when dining out in Perlis. There is a good selection of restaurants and fast food outlets in Kangar. Seafood is very popular especially in Kuala Perlis where the proximity to the sea means that excellent Ikan Bakar or grilled fish can be found easily. Visitors can also try out the Laksa Kuala Perlis. It is made of fish gravy and topped with boiled eggs, cucumber, onion and herbs. Let your sense of adventure rule while you savour the delights of Perlis!

Permission to enter and guide services are compulsory.

## GUNUNG PERLIS, WANG KELIAN

The 733m high Gunung Perlis is the northernmost point of Peninsular Malaysia. One of the main attractions of the Perlis State Park, this mountain offers outdoor enthusiasts a tranquil setting to enjoy nature at its best. An average hike to the top takes between three to five hours. Visitors are required to register and engage the services of a qualified guide at the park's visitor centre.

+604 945 7898 forestry.perlis.gov.my

WANG KELIAN VIEWPOINT, KAKI BUKIT Enjoy unobstructed views of Perlis and its surroundings from a height of 304 metres! To get to Wang Kelian Viewpoint, visitors will have to park their vehicles at the foothill and take a short hike to the top. Once at the viewpoint, you will be surprised to see the horizon stretching as far as Lake Timah Tasoh and beyond. Feeling peckish? Enjoy tasty local food at the stalls here.

+604 976 5966 forestry.perlis.gov.my

## WANG BURMA CAVE

Situated within the Perlis State Park, Wang Burma Cave is one of the most popular caves for spelunking. The cave has stalactites and stalagmites resembling pillars, curtains and cascades which were formed over millions of years. Explore the caves at leisure, or climb, crawl and squeeze between narrow passages and muddy tunnels. Bring out the thrillseeker in you! Permission to enter and the services of a guide are required.

+604 945 7898

forestry.perlis.gov.my

## THE ZON DUTY-FREE COMPLEX, PADANG BESAR

Located at the northern border of Malaysia, this is one of the most popular shopping spots for locals and visitors from around the region. The complex offers a wide selection of duty-free items such as chocolates, perfumes, fashion apparels, luggage, electrical items and household goods. It is situated in Padang Besar, about 32km from Kangar.

+6019 491 3698 www.zon.com.my

## FRIM CANOPY WALKWAY

Nature lovers will enjoy a visit to the Forest Research Institute Malaysia (FRIM) Canopy Walkway. The walkway spans 100 metres and is suspended 17 metres above ground level, giving visitors an exciting adventure and fantastic views of the surrounding vista. It is supported by iron-based towers for safety. The FRIM Canopy Walk is open from 9.00am to 4.00pm from Monday to Friday.

+604 945 6235 www.frim.gov.my

## SHOPPING IN PADANG BESAR

Accessible by road or rail, the town of Padang Besar has long been a bargain hunter's paradise. Begin your shopping spree at the two-storey Padang Besar Arcade and browse through the abundant supply of goods, from handicrafts, household items to fabrics and other products. Miscellaneous shops and open-air flea markets add to the air of excitement and gaiety here, offering a wide selection of foodstuff and products.

## GUA KELAM RECREATIONAL PARK (DARK CAVES), KAKI BUKIT

Stretching 370m long, Gua Kelam is one of the most distinctive limestone caves in Malaysia. It is accessible via an eight-foot-wide wooden suspension bridge. Visitors can see remnants of the tin mining operation that was once carried out here. A wooden walkway hangs suspended throughout the length of the underground cavern and a subterranean stream flows through, adding an air of mysticism to the caves. Gua Kelam Recreational Park is situated about 26km from Kangar.

+604 945 6917

forestry.perlis.gov.my

## DATARAN EMPANGAN TIMAH TASOH (LAKE TIMAH TASOH)

Situated within the Perlis State Park, the scenic Lake Timah Tasoh was built as a dam in 1992 to supply water and to prevent flooding. The lake area is a sanctuary for migratory birds, providing them with much-needed food and shelter. Further afield, the Bukit Chabang range runs in the north and the Nakawan range in the south, forming a breathtaking backdrop. In the evenings, the lake is a popular spot to view the sunset. Accommodation is available.

## +6011 3308 6889

## GALERI DIRAJA (PERLIS ROYAL GALLERY), ARAU

The Royal Gallery showcases an impressive collection of the state's royal regalia, portraits, ceremonial court items, ancient weapons and state gifts which were accorded to the royal family. The gallery is situated in the royal town of Arau, about 12km from Kangar. Visitors must obtain written permission from the gallery's management before a visit.

## +604 986 4835

dymm.perlis.gov.my

KUALA PERLIS SEAFOOD, KUALA PERLIS Kuala Perlis is a small fishing town situated about 14km from Kangar. The fisherfolk unload their catch at this small town every day, making it famous for its fresh and cheap seafood. Visitors can enjoy a variety of seafood and other dishes at the numerous restaurants and stalls found around the town and along the shoreline.

+604 976 1088 pkenps.gov.my

KOTA KAYANG MUSEUM, BUKIT KAYANG This museum gives a good introduction to the history of Perlis. Visitors can begin their tour at the display section before moving on to the artefacts and relics. Among the artefacts displayed here include the state's royal regalia, Neolithic stone tools, document replicas, royal genealogical charts from the time of Prophet Muhammad, weapons, brassware and coins. The museum grounds used to house the ancient Indera Kayangan Fort, a palace and a ceremonial hall.

+604 977 0027 www.jmm.gov.my

## MASJID AL-HUSSAIN (AL-HUSSAIN MOSQUE), KUALA PERLIS

This beautiful floating mosque is an icon in the seaside town of Kuala Perlis, and a source of pride for the state. Adopting a fresh, tropical architectural look, the walls of the mosque are not painted, but decorated with different types of rock, quartz, granite, marble and pebbles. Another unique feature is that at prayer times, a beam of light is shone, indicating prayer times to the fishermen at sea.

## SUPERFRUITS VALLEY, CHUPING

We all know that fruits have amazing health benefits but imagine what superfruits could do. Head to this 10-acre orchard in Chuping to taste not one, but nine superfruits! The fruits range from figs, gac, lemons and passionfruit to mulberry and more. Bursting with nutrition, these fruits are rich in antioxidants, fibre, vitamins and minerals. Even better, the fruits are grown locally and naturally without any pesticides. Don't miss the chance to try freshly plucked fruits and taste the delicious fig ice cream!

+6012 328 3280

www.superfruitsvalley.com

## BUKIT KETERI

Calling all climbers! Adventure-seekers should try this 131-metre hill that is ranked as one of the toughest in Asia. The karst limestone formation is reputed to be about 250 million years old. It has an incredibly huge cave chamber as big as a football field. Bukit Keteri's most popular feature is the Belly Button Window, which attracts climbers from around the world for the challenge it presents and scenic views. Bukit Keteri lies about 10km from Kangar. Climbing packages are available with instructor and guide services.

## +6012 946 6569 (Perlis Climber)

## BUKIT CHABANG MARI

Said to remind travellers of Lauterbrunnen in Switzerland, Bukit Chabang Mari's scenic vista is made up of its iconic twin hills, lots of greenery, a goat farm, campsite and fish ponds. Get your cameras ready!

## KAMPUNG WARNA WARNI, KUALA PERLIS

Literally meaning 'colourful village', this place lifts the spirits with its bright and lively hues. Perfect for Insta-worthy shots, Kampung Warna Warni is essentially a combination of seven neighbouring villages that have been transformed into a trendy tourist spot. The sights are made even more interesting with creative nuances by home-owners such as decorative items made of repurposed materials, themed colours, insightful quotes, street art and other delightful personal touches.
