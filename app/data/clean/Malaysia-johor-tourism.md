## X-PARK SUNWAY ISKANDAR

X Park is the country's first theme park dedicated to motorized and non-motorized extreme sports. Spread over 40 acres, X Park offers over 20 different activities that suit various ages and preferences. Get your adrenalin fix here with Flying Fox, Motocross, Go Kart, ATV, High Rope, Bubbleball Futsal, Skate Park and Paintball. Visitors also have ample choices for milder pursuits such as Aquawalk, Kayaking, Swan Boat Paddling and Glamping.

## MUZIUM TOKOH JOHOR

Muzium Tokoh gives an insight on the illustrious list of Johor's leaders and dignitaries, especially the Royal Family of Johor. Visitors can view a range of artefacts such as documents of the royal lineage. Housed in the Dato' Jaafar Building, the museum has a long history dating back to 1893. It was the official residence of the first Chief Minister of Johor, the late Dato' Jaafar bin Muhammad. The design concept of the building is similar to the Hardwick Hall in Derbyshire.

## JOHOR BAHRU CHINESE HERITAGE MUSEUM

Johor Bahru Chinese Heritage Museum is housed in a restored four-storey shoplot, belonging to the prominent merchant Quok Keng Kang. The building was converted into the Chinese General Association during the Japanese Occupation in 1942, before it was renamed the Johor Bahru Tiong-Hua Association (JBTHA) in 1948. The building was renovated and officiated by the late Sultan Ismail in 1966. It was officially opened as a heritage museum in 2009.

## +607-224 9633

## SULTAN IBRAHIM BUILDING

A famous landmark in Johor Bahru, this building features an architecture that combines local and English elements. It functioned as a fortress and command centre during the Second World War and served as the administration centre of the state government until it was relocated to Kota Iskandar.

## SULTAN ABU BAKAR MOSQUE Johor Bahru

Built in 1892, this beautiful mosque is situated on a hill overlooking the Straits of Tebrau. Its design is influenced by Victorian and Mughal Indian architecture. Simple ornamentation on the arches resemble the Man Mandir Palace in Gwalior, India, while the twin columns at the façade depict Victorian architecture. The main prayer hall is rectangular in shape with a five-ridge roof known as 'Bumbung Perabung'. This mosque was commissioned by the late Sultan Abu Bakar, who was famed as the 'Father of Modern Johor'. Visitors are advised to dress modestly while visiting the mosque area.

## Malaysia

Situated in the heart of Southeast Asia, Malaysia is a vibrant destination with a diversity of cultures and traditions. This tropical paradise boasts pristine jungles and verdant national parks that are teeming with numerous species of birds and wildlife.

Malaysia's sun-drenched islands and beaches are home to a stunning range of marine life. Its natural wealth is complemented by magnificent modern landmarks and heritage buildings. There is something to charm every visitor, be it a history-lover, nature-enthusiast, sun-seeker or an avid shopper. Explore the length and breadth of the country to discover the many attractions of Malaysia!

## Johor

Johor is Malaysia's southern gateway. Linked to Singapore via the Causeway (Tambak Johor) and the Second Link (LINKEDUA), its strategic location leads to its encouraging growth over the years, making it one of the country's most developed states. The state's wealth of natural attractions includes verdant rainforests, cascading waterfalls, thriving wetlands and mesmerising tropical islands. Comprising eight main districts, the state's vast landscape comprises sweeping plantations, well-manicured golf lawns, tranquil fishing villages, lush nature reserves and golden sandy beaches.

## GETTING THERE

## By Air

You can fly to Johor from several destinations in Malaysia. Johor is also accessible from Sabah and Sarawak by air. Senai International Airport is situated 32 km from Johor Bahru.

## By Road

Johor Bahru is 330 km or a four-hour drive from Kuala Lumpur via the North-South Expressway. Both the JohorSingapore Causeway and the Second Link Expressway connect Singapore to Johor as well as the rest of the peninsula. Bus services to Johor from major cities and towns in the peninsula are available throughout the day. Tickets must be purchased early during peak seasons.

## By Rail

Keretapi Tanah Melayu Berhad (KTMB) or the national railway connects Johor to other states in the peninsula and also Singapore. JB Sentral Railway Station is the southernmost station in the country. Other stations include Kempas Baru, Kluang, Kulai, Labis, Paloh, Renggam and Segamat.

## By Sea

From Singapore, Tanjung Belungkor is the entrance point for ferries. Ferry services are also available from Batam and Tanjung Pinang in Indonesia to Johor Bahru International Ferry Terminal.

## SHOPPING

Johor has an extensive range of shopping malls, craft bazaars, duty-free outlets, hypermarkets and night markets. Many of these outlets can be found in Johor Bahru and other towns. Select from a huge variety of local and imported products ranging from designer labels, fashion accessories, electrical appliances to ceramics and handicrafts.

## DINING

Don't forget to sample the mouthwatering spread of delicacies such as Laksa Johor, Nasi Briyani Gam, Lontong and Mee Bandung . Other not-to-be-missed specialties include Mee Rebus, Kacang Pool, Asam Pedas and Otak-Otak . Seafood lovers should head to Kukup, about 10 minutes drive from Tanjung Piai, for a feast of seafood delights.

## GETTING AROUND

You can book a taxi at the Larkin Taxi Station, bus stations, hotels and shopping complexes. It is advisable to request that the taxi meter be switched on or agree on the fare before you begin the ride. Alternatively, rent a car from car rental companies at the airport and bus stations. E-hailing services are also available in Johor.

## PLACES OF INTEREST

## NASUHA HERBS AND SPICES PARADISE

This 1,000-hectare spice and herb farm cultivates hundreds of medicinal plants and is believed to be among the biggest herb and spice plantations in Asia. Nasuha Herbs and Spices Paradise is located a few kilometres from Pagoh town in the district of Muar and offers spa treatments as well as accommodation.

+606-973 7231 nasuha.business.site

## ADVENTURE WATERPARK DESARU COAST Bandar Penawar

Set against the backdrop of a traditional Malaysian fishing village, Adventure Waterpark Desaru Coast offers unique rides including the first-ever water coaster in the region and one of the biggest wave pools in the world. The waterpark features more than 20 wet and dry rides, slides and attractions, with something for everyone. Spread across 21 acres, the entire Desaru Coast development

boasts luxury hotels, exclusive restaurants and a championship golf course.

+607-878 3800/28

www.desarucoast.com

## UK FARM Kluang

Come and experience modern farm life at the 100-acre UK Farm, which is believed to be the largest goat and sheep farm in Malaysia with a breeding capacity of 4,000. Activities here include guided tours of the farm and visits to the Jakun indigenous community village as well as the Mini Zoo.

+607-759 7555 / 5018

www.ukfarm.com.my

## ZENXIN ORGANIC PARK

## Kluang

Enjoy fresh, organically grown vegetables and fruits at Zenxin Organic Park. You can also take part in recreational activities such as cycling, join a walking tour around the park or feed fish at the pond. Other attractions include a park café, flower nursery and a farm mart, a one-stop organic food shop.

+607-759 5196

www.zenxin.com.my

## LEGOLAND MALAYSIA RESORT Iskandar Puteri

The first-of-its-kind in Asia and the first international park in Malaysia, Legoland offers an enjoyable experience with thrilling rides, interactive displays and themed areas. Visit all of its seven attractions - each offering different thrills and rides.

+607-597 8888

www.legoland.my

## ENDAU-ROMPIN NATIONAL PARK

## Kluang

Straddling across two states, the Endau-Rompin National Park consists of an area of approximately 92,000 hectares and encompasses the rivers of Endau and Rompin. It is known to be the sanctuary of the largest surviving population of Sumatran

Rhinoceros in Peninsular Malaysia.

+607-788 2812

www.johornationalparks.gov.my

## KOREF DESARU LEISURE FARM

Situated about 45 minutes drive from Kluang, in northeastern Johor, this unique farm endeavours to grow agricultural produce using sustainable methods. It utilises integrated organic farming methods to produce rice, livestock, fish, fruits, vegetables and promotes itself as an agro as well as eco-tourism destination. Koref Desaru Leisure Farm was certified as an organic rice farm in 2006, the first in the country.

+012-720 8158

http://desaruorganic.com

## LABIS SUNRISE FARM Kota Tinggi

Labis Sunrise Farm, as the name implies, offers the first ray of sunshine from atop the misty mountain. Spread across 150 acres, the farm has a wide variety of fruits including the gac fruit and rose apples. Labis Sunrise Farm is reputed to have the largest rose apple farm in Johor. Enjoy camping, go on a farm tour or try out activities such as rubber tapping. This nature getaway offers the perfect remedy for those who need a break from the hustle and bustle of city life.

+6011-1064 3688

www.labissunrisefarm.com

## JOHOR PREMIUM OUTLETS

This shoppers' paradise is located in a unique outdoor setting and offers designer and branded items such as apparel, footwear as well as fashion accessories at unbelievable prices. Since opening in December 2011, it has drawn thousands of shoppers from both sides of the Causeway.

+607-661 8888

www.premiumoutlets.com.my

## PULAU KUKUP NATIONAL PARK Pontian

Pulau Kukup is a 647-hectare, uninhabited mangrove island. A RAMSAR site (Wetland of International Importance), it is surrounded by 800 hectares of mudflats and is reputedly one of the largest-ofits-kind. It was declared a national park in 1997, in order to preserve the island's diverse variety of wetland flora and fauna.

+607-696 9355

www.johornationalparks.gov.my

## TANJUNG PIAI NATIONAL PARK

## Pontian

Tanjung Piai National Park encompasses over 926 hectares of rainforest, including 526 hectares of coastal mangroves. Among its inhabitants are dusky leaf monkeys, long-tailed macaques, mud skippers as well as rare bird species and migratory birds. A monument here marks Tanjung Piai as the southernmost tip of Mainland Asia.

+607-696 9762

www.johornationalparks.gov.my

## PUTERI HARBOUR Iskandar Puteri

Puteri Harbour is among the seven signature developments in the city of Nusajaya - the largest fully integrated urban development in Southeast Asia. Situated along the Straits of Johor, the 688acre jewel of Nusajaya features the southernmost marina on the Asian mainland and comprises residential as well as commercial developments.

+607-530 2122

www.nusajayacity.com

## FOREST CITY Gelang Patah

Made up of four man-made islands, Forest City is a new smart and green development in the state. Utilising cutting-edge technology, the futuristic city comprises hotels, shopping outlets, restaurants, a golf resort, verdant parks and idyllic sea views. Enjoy a meal at the numerous stylish, trendy cafes and restaurants and take in the beauty of this urban gem.

## PASIR GUDANG KITE MUSEUM Pasir Gudang

Discover all things related to kites in the Pasir Gudang Kite Museum. The first-of-its-kind in the country, this unique museum showcases the traditional Malay kite known as wau . On display here are handmade kites of various colours and designs. You can also watch a video on kite-making.

+607-252 7796 www.mbpg.gov.my/ms

## MUAR RIVER CRUISE

Situated about two hours drive from Johor Bahru, Muar is a historical town and the capital city for the district of Muar. The town is also known as Bandar Maharani and has the distinction of being Johor's royal town. Muar's riverbank is the site of numerous interesting sights and scenes, from distinguished buildings to beautiful views of the sunset. One of the best ways to take in the views is by the Muar River Cruise. Enjoy unobstructed views of the Sultan Ibrahim Mosque, Sultan Ismail Bridge and Dataran Tanjung Emas which is famous for its seafood. The cruise departs from Jetty Tanjung Emas.

+6013-669 2263

facebook.com/rivercruiseinmuar

## SUNGAI JOHOR AND SUNGAI LEBAM FIREFLIES

## Kota Tinggi

Feast your eyes on the spectacular light displays by the firefly colonies of Sungai Johor and Sungai Lebam. One of Johor's top night attractions, these unique insects live and feed on the berembang trees that grow along the riverbanks.

+6012-783 3723 / +6012-789 6224

https://alisashahana.wixsite.com/bujangfirefly m.facebook.com/Kota-Tinggi-Firefly-Valley-Leisure-Park

## DESARU FRUIT FARM Kota Tinggi

Don't miss the unique opportunity to explore a tropical fruit orchard. Within the 100-acre Desaru Fruit Farm, visitors will find a mouth-watering variety of fruits such as durian, rambutan, mangosteen, pineapple, papaya, passion fruit and rose apple. Orchard tours are available for visitors interested in learning more about these local delicacies.

## https://dff.world

## KAMPUNG SUNGAI MELAYU ECO TOURISM VILLAGE Iskandar Puteri

Kampung Sungai Melayu is a century-old Malay village that has been designated as an 'ecotourism village' by the state government. Surrounded by tropical vegetation, the village is an oasis of tranquility and greenery. Birdwatching enthusiasts will be happy to spot the variety of birdlife in the area. Take a short boat ride to the floating mussel farm or enjoy a mangrove tour for an interesting insight into the area's biodiversity. Overnight visitors can enjoy a more immersive experience through a homestay programme.

+6019-704 5904

http://kampungsungaimelayu.blogspot.com

## TAMAN LAUT SULTAN ISKANDAR

Located in Mersing, the marine park is made up of a cluster of six islands - Pulau Aur, Pulau Tinggi, Pulau Pemanggil, Pulau Besar, Pulau Rawa and Pulau Sibu. Covering over 8,000 hectares, the marine park is characterised by crystal-clear waters, powdery-white sandy beaches and a diverse marine life. Spend a relaxing day on the beach or take part in water sports.

+607-266 1301

www.johornationalparks.gov.my

## PULAU RAWA

## Mersing

Pulau Rawa mesmerises with its crystalline blue and emerald waters. You can choose to relax on its sunny beaches and secluded coves, indulge in water sports such as windsurfing, sailing and kayaking or get close to marine life by snorkelling and diving.

+607-799 1204 / 1205 www.rawaislandresort.com
