## PLACES OF INTEREST

## NEGERI SEMBILAN STATE MUSEUM COMPLEX

Established in 1953, the State Museum provides an overview of the state's rich history and culture. Housed in an old wooden palace which has been restored to its original grandeur, the museum showcases a varied range of exhibits, including ancient weaponry as well as brass and silverware used by the Royal families. Next to the museum is the Negeri Sembilan House, a fine example of Minangkabau architecture.

lmns.ns.gov.my

## THEN SZE KHOON TEMPLE

Perched majestically on Bukit Jung, 3km from the town centre, the Then Sze Khoon Temple is believed to be more than a hundred years old. This popular Taoist temple is also known as the 'Centipede Temple' due to the many centipedes that thrive on its grounds. The temple features many colourful life-sized sculptures of Chinese deities, a pagoda, koi pond, and garden.

## SEREMBAN LAKE GARDEN

The Seremban Lake Garden covers 10 acres of landscaped grounds surrounded by tropical trees, plants, and flowers. Here, visitors can enjoy a stroll through the garden's relaxing green spaces or get active on the jogging track. Just 2km from the town centre, this popular recreational area boasts two lakes, one of which has a floating stage where entertainment programmes are held regularly.

www.mbs.gov.my

## MASJID NEGERI SEMBILAN (NEGERI SEMBILAN STATE MOSQUE)

Constructed between 1967 and 1970, Negeri Sembilan State Mosque sits atop a hill overlooking the Seremban Lake Garden and downtown Seremban. A fusion of modern and traditional Minangkabau architectural styles, the mosque was designed with nine pillars, which extend beyond its roofline, to represent the original nine districts of the state.

jheains.ns.gov.my

## SRI BALATHANDAYUTHAPANI TEMPLE

Situated right in the heart of town, the Sri Balathandayuthapani Temple has been in existence since 1895, making it one of the oldest Hindu temples in Negeri Sembilan. Spread over 65,000 sqft, this historical temple is adorned with intricate architectural details and religious art, complemented by landscaped gardens and cascading waterfalls.

www.mbs.gov.my

## SEREMBAN ORCHID PARK

Located within the grounds of the Seremban Lake Garden, the Seremban Orchid Park is built on four acres of carefully landscaped hilly terrain. An interesting array of orchids, both wild and hybrid,can be found here. With its wellthought-out display areas and serene surroundings, the park is a great place for visitors to enjoy and learn more about orchids.

www.mbs.gov.my

## LORONG SENI SEREMBAN (SEREMBAN STREET ART)

The Seremban Art Street can be found along Jalan Dato' Bandar Tunggal, Jalan Yam Tuan, Jalan Tuanku Munawir, and Jalan Tuanku Munawir all the way up to Benteng Walk Seremban. The 250m-long street features a variety of interesting murals with various genre concepts such as graffiti, doodles, avant-garde posters, heritage paintings, and several other elements. Each mural is painted with its own story, making Seremban a popular destination for social media enthusiasts.

www.mbs.gov.my

## JELITA OSTRICH FARM

The 4.2-hectare Jelita Ostrich Farm is the first ostrich farm in Malaysia. Located 10km from Seremban, this show farm provides a rare opportunity to observe ostriches up close in a natural environment. Guided tours are available to introduce visitors to these flightless birds, including the various aspects of ostrich breeding and rearing.

yogstrich.wixsite.com/jelitaostrichfarm

## STARFRESH AGRO PARK

Getting back to nature is easy at the Starfresh Agro Park, just 9km from the Seremban town centre. The park features 40 acres of tropical vegetation, fruit trees, and herbal plants, as well as a variety of domestic farm animals. Visitors have a choice of leisure, experiential learning, and team building packages, ranging in duration from one to two days. Popular activities here are farm visits, river rafting, and paintball. Dormitory-style accommodation and campsites are available for overnight stays. Both western and local fare can be savoured at restaurants located within the

park itself.

www.starfresh.com.my

## MASJID SRI SENDAYAN (SRI SENDAYAN MOSQUE)

The stunning beauty of Sri Sendayan Mosque will truly captivate you when you visit. When the sun shines, the mosque's bright white color simply glows. Don't miss the chance to admire the amazing wood carvings on the walls of the prayer hall, the beautiful gold plating, calligraphy, and the unique tile blocks near the pulpit. The interior ceiling was painted by hand, and the dome sparkles with 24k

gold paint.

www.masjidsrisendayan.com.my

## Malaysia

Immerse yourself in the vibrant heart of Southeast Asia with a visit to Malaysia. Experience the beauty of pristine jungles, verdant national parks, and sun-drenched islands teeming with marine life. Explore modern landmarks and heritage buildings, while indulging in the rich blend of various cultures and traditions. Whether you're a history-lover, nature enthusiast, sun-seeker, or avid shopper, Malaysia has something for everyone. Embark on a journey to discover the country's many alluring attractions!

## Negeri Sembilan

The name Negeri Sembilan literally means 'Land of the Nine States', a reference to the nine districts that used to make up the state. Its capital is Seremban while the royal town is Seri Menanti. Its ubiquitous Minangkabau cultural heritage dates back to the 15th century. It is the only state in Malaysia where the Adat Perpatih, a set of customs based on the ethnic group's matrilineal social system, is practised to this day. The Minangkabau influence can also be seen in the distinctive buffalo-horn-shaped roofs of many iconic buildings throughout the state. With its laid-back ambience, varied landscapes, and a vibrant mix of beaches, parks, and attractions, Negeri Sembilan is a great choice for a relaxing getaway.

## GETTING THERE

Negeri Sembilan is easily accessible via several major highways, including the North-South Expressway (NSE) and Kajang-Seremban Highway (LEKAS). Its state capital, Seremban, is about 68km from Kuala Lumpur and 270km from Johor Bahru, using the NSE. Seremban is also the southern terminal of the electrified commuter rail network, KTM Komuter, which connects the town to Kuala Lumpur and the Klang Valley.

The nearest airport to Negeri Sembilan is the Kuala Lumpur International Airport, 57km away. Direct bus services are available between the airport and Seremban. There are ferry connections between Port Dickson and Dumai in Indonesia.

## GETTING AROUND

Negeri Sembilan is well served by buses and taxis plying both local and inter-town routes. Seremban's main public transport hub is Terminal One, located in the town centre. You can get a taxi at the Seremban Railway Terminal, the Terminal One, and the Port Dickson Bus Terminal. Bus services are available from the Terminal One Bus Station in Seremban to towns like Kuala Pilah, Kuala Kelawang, Port Dickson, Nilai, and Tampin. E-hailing services are also readily available and always a reliable choice.

## SHOPPING

From lively night markets to modern malls, Negeri Sembilan offers a plethora of shopping options, especially in Seremban and Nilai. One of the most popular shopping destinations here is the Nilai 3 Wholesale Centre, the largest wholesale centre in Malaysia. Local handicrafts and souvenirs can be purchased at various craft centres around the state, including the Negeri Sembilan Craft

Complex.

## DINING

While an array of Asian and western food can be found everywhere in Seremban and other major towns, Rembau is the place to go for authentic Minang fare. Traditional Negeri Sembilan food is generally known to be on the spicy side because of the liberal use of chillies in addition to coconut milk. A signature cooking style is masak lemak cili api which literally means 'cooked in coconut milk and bird's eye chillies'. The state's specialities include ikan keli berlada (catfish in chilli paste) and rendang (dry meat curry) served with lemang (glutinous rice and coconut milk cooked in hollow bamboo segments).

## TAMAN NEGERI KENABOI (KENABOI STATE PARK) | JELEBU

Kenaboi State Park is an eco-adventure destination located in the Jelebu district, about 100km from Seremban. It is home to Mount Besar Hantu, the highest peak in Negeri Sembilan, and Lata Kijang, one of the tallest waterfalls in Peninsular Malaysia. Visitors can enjoy various nature activities, and the park offers facilities such as a multipurpose hall, campsites, gazebos, restrooms, and parking.

forestry.ns.gov.my

## PORT DICKSON | PORT DICKSON

Affectionately referred to as PD, the seaside town of Port Dickson is a popular weekend destination on Peninsular Malaysia's southwestern coast. Its sun-kissed beaches, lined by towering coconut trees and shady casuarinas, provide the perfect backdrop for a relaxing beach holiday or picnic. The better-known beaches here are Teluk Kemang and Blue Lagoon, where holidaymakers can enjoy myriad water sports and beach activities.

www.mppd.gov.my

## ARMY MUSEUM

## | PORT DICKSON

This award-winning army museum traces the illustrious history of the Malaysian Armed Forces through several galleries, featuring military artefacts, memorabilia, and more. A rich source of historical information, the museum provides a unique insight into Malaysia's proud military heritage. Outdoor exhibits include a memorial fountain and decommissioned military vehicles such as planes, tanks, and artillery guns.

www.mppd.gov.my

## THE ROYAL MUSEUM OF SERI MENANTI | SERI MENANTI

Royal Museum of Seri Menanti is a former royal palace and one of the last remaining wooden palaces in Malaysia. It was built out of wood in 1908 without the use of a single nail. This five-storey building houses a collection of royal regalia as well as various costumes, furniture, weapons, and historical paraphernalia. It is located in the royal town of Seri Menanti, some 50km from Seremban.

lmns.ns.gov.my

## HOMESTAYS OF NEGERI SEMBILAN

The homestay programme presents guests with a unique opportunity to experience first hand authentic rural living in Negeri Sembilan. Visitors will get to immerse themselves in the daily life and activities of the host family, from playing traditional games to helping prepare local delicacies. Various homestays are already mapped out for visitors to get the full range of an idyllic village experience.

## ULU BENDUL ECO-PARK FOREST

## | KUALA PILAH

Situated 16km from Seremban, the Ulu Bendul Eco-Park Forest provides an ideal setting for picnicking, camping, and other leisure activities. Among the attractions here are a cascading waterfall and man-made rock pool, making it a great spot to cool off on hot days. A starting point for hiking up Mount Angsi, Ulu Bendul also boasts several shorter trails that are perfect for jungle-trekking. Not too far away are also other attractions such as the Ulu Bendul Snake Park, where you can learn more about these intriguing reptiles.

www.forestry.gov.my/negerisembilan

## ADAT MUSEUM

## | JELEBU

The Adat Museum is a must-visit for those who are curious about Minangkabau culture. The museum houses a comprehensive collection of Minang artefacts and archival materials. Displayed in five main exhibition areas, the exhibits range from ceremonial costumes, traditional musical instruments and buffalo horn-shaped headdresses, to detailed explanations of the matrilineal society's Adat Perpatih customs.

www.jmm.gov.my/en/museum/customs-museum

## GUNUNG DATUK RECREATIONAL FOREST

## | REMBAU

A popular hiking spot, the Gunung Datuk Recreational Forest is situated just 35km from Seremban. It is covered in hill dipterocarp forest which provides a habitat for a diverse array of plant and animal life. The trail up Mount Datuk is a relatively short one and will take about two to three hours to complete. For the convenience of visitors, the forest park also features chalets and rest huts. Please check with the State Forestry Department for permit requirements.

www.forestry.gov.my/negerisembilan

## REMBAU MUSEUM (REPLICA OF RAJA MELEWAR'S PALACE) | REMBAU

Just 12km from Rembau town, the museum is actually a replica of the 300-year-old palace of Raja Melewar, Negeri Sembilan's first ruler. Several galleries in the museum offer a glimpse into Rembau's rich historical past, the Adat Perpatih customs that are unique to the state as well as artefacts on Raja Melewar.

lmns.ns.gov.my

## FORT LUKUT & LUKUT MUSEUM | PORT DICKSON

About 30km from Seremban lies Lukut Fort, a historical monument that was built in 1826 to protect the tin-rich town of Lukut in its heyday. Here, visitors can enjoy a sweeping view of Lukut and explore the fort's ancient ruins. A short distance away, the Lukut Museum pays tribute to the glorious past of Lukut and its fort. Exhibits here include artefacts unearthed at the fort as well as a showcase of Minangkabau traditions and customs.

www.jmm.gov.my/en/museum/lukut-museum

## TERATAK ZA'BA

## (HOUSE OF PENDETA ZA'BA) | JEMPOL

This memorial house acts as a repository of documents, memorabilia, and other research materials chronicling the life and times of Tan Sri Zainal Abidin bin Ahmad, also known as Za'ba. An icon in the world of Malay literature and philosophy, Za'ba made many notable contributions to Malaysia's economic, political, and social development. The house is located in Za'ba's birthplace in Bukit Kerdas, about 50km from Seremban.

lmns.ns.gov.my

## PD FOREST SUNGAI MENYALA | PORT DICKSON

Designated as the first eco-edu tourism park in Malaysia, this 1,300-hectare forest reserve is a wonderful place to learn about the forest and its rich biodiversity. Nature lovers can explore the different habitats here through a network of trails and boardwalks. The park offers a comprehensive range of facilities, including accommodation, a showcase of aborigine culture and forestry specimens, as well as lecture halls.

www.mppd.gov.my

## TELUK KEMANG OBSERVATORY

(BAITUL HILAL COMPLEX) | PORT DICKSON

Also known as the Baitul Hilal Complex, this observatory is located 10km south of Port Dickson on a 1.2-hectare site. It is equipped with one of the largest and most advanced telescopes in Southeast Asia. Several vintage telescopes and astrology artefacts are also housed here.

bctkpd.com

## GUNUNG ANGSI (MOUNT ANGSI)

## | KUALA PILAH

The 825m-high Mount Angsi is located within the Ulu Bendul Eco-Park Forest in Kuala Pilah, about 16km from Seremban. Due to its well-developed trails and adequate signage, Mount Angsi is a relatively easy mountain to hike up. The summit, which takes three to four hours to reach, offers a great view of Seremban. The mountain is accessible via Ulu Bendul and Bukit Putus, 3km away. Please check with the State Forestry Department for permit requirements.

www.forestry.gov.my/negerisembilan

## GUNUNG TAMPIN (MOUNT TAMPIN) | TAMPIN

Mount Tampin stands at a height of 765m on the southern end of the Titiwangsa range. Situated within the Tampin Forest Reserve about 2km from Tampin town, the mountain is covered in a hill dipterocarp forest that's rich in flora and fauna. Various trails provide hikers with scenic views of gently undulating terrain, lush greenery, and rocky outcrops. There are also designated recreational areas with rest huts and a children's playground.

www.forestry.gov.my/negerisembilan

## EAGLE RANCH SPORTS PARADISE | PORT DICKSON

The cowboy-themed Eagle Ranch Resort is set amidst 32 acres of rugged surroundings with horse trails and eagles soaring overhead, hence the name. The resort offers all kinds of fun-filled activities, including horse riding, archery, go-karting, canoeing, paintball, flying fox, and sea rafting. Accommodation options here range from log cabins and paddock chalets to Native American-style tepee tents. Guests will also get to savour a wide array of western and local fare. Eagle Ranch is located about 24km from the Port Dickson town centre.

www.eagleranch.com.my

## TITI ECO FARM

## | KUALA KLAWANG

Titi Eco Farm comprises an organic farm, resort, and in-farm factory. Sprawled across 40 acres, the farm also features fruit and herb gardens, fish ponds, a poultry farm, and recreational areas. The farm's resort is a relaxing retreat where guests can immerse themselves in nature and enjoy the organic food grown here. The farm also produces a wide range of certified organic produce which can be purchased at its organic mart.
